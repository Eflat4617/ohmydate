# Design System: OhMyDate

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "A Calm Dark Dashboard With One Lime Signal"**

OhMyDate opens on a dashboard. The ground is near-black with a faint green cast; on it sit a few large cards of related sizes on one grid, with generous dark space between and inside them. Each card says one thing: a small quiet title, one big light number, and a chart or a short list under it. Text is off-white in three steps. Lime is the one signal: the primary button, the current nav item, today, now and next, and the leading series of every chart. Cyan is the second data colour and the "second kind" on calendars, orange the third; red is kept for danger and for what is overdue.

This system replaced the paper "텐미닛 플래너" system on 2026-11-13 (HOLO-01), after the user saw the direction they had wanted all along. What carries over is the product: one next action read first, proposals told apart from what is confirmed, time shown so it can be compared.

The arrangement is the point. Sidebar to canvas is 240px to the rest; the top bar is one quiet line; cards sit on a 12-column grid in threes, twos or one; one lime thing per view. A screen is composed from OhMyDate's own data so the page has that balance, never by filling a template.

A light scheme shares every token: a pale ground, white cards, ink text. There lime stays a fill behind dark text, and lime as text becomes a deep green. The theme is the user's choice (다크 by default, 라이트, 시스템), not the operating system's.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-black ground with a green cast, three surface steps, hairlines in white at low alpha.
- The card is the unit: 20px corners, a hairline edge, a faint top-left sheen in the dark scheme.
- One big light number per card (weight 300, tabular), over a small quiet label.
- One lime signal; cyan and orange as data; red only for danger and overdue.
- Pretendard, bundled; headings medium, body 16px.
- Hand-written SVG charts with a text alternative each.
- Motion is short and functional; nothing moves under reduced motion.
- Three layers: ambient light, solid content, and liquid glass for everything that floats.
- One screen where things float freely: 할 일, a canvas of to-dos and folders.
- One glass object, made by this project (three.js, lazily loaded, with a still image behind it), in at most one or two places.

## Colors

Every value is an OKLCH custom property in `src/styles/tokens.css`; no raw colour appears in any other stylesheet or in any Vue file (the glass scene's own light colours are the one exception, inside `glassScene.js`). The dark scheme is `:root`; the light scheme redefines the same names under `:root[data-scheme='light']` (the `-light` entries in the frontmatter).

### How a theme reaches the page
`src/theme/theme.ts` keeps two attributes on `<html>`:

- `data-theme` is what the user chose: `dark`, `light` or `system`.
- `data-scheme` is what is shown: `dark` or `light`.

A part of the page can ask for the dark scheme by itself with the class `.scheme-dark` (Focus calm mode); nothing else forces a scheme.

Stylesheets read only `data-scheme`. No stylesheet has a `prefers-color-scheme` query; the media query is consulted in `theme.ts`, and only while the choice is `system`. The choice is stored per owner through `session/ownerStorage.ts` (slot `themePref`). `main.ts` applies it before it mounts anything, and the entry script is `blocking="render"`, so Chromium paints no frame before the theme is on the root. In a browser without `blocking="render"` a 라이트 user can see one empty dark frame.

### Surfaces
- **Ground** (`--color-ground`): the page, the sidebar, the top bar.
- **Surface** (`--color-surface`): a card.
- **Raised** (`--color-raised`): a chart callout, and the opaque form of glass (`--glass-solid`). What floats is glass (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Inset** (`--color-inset`): a well inside a card (the tracker's timer) and every field.
- **Fill** / **Fill 2** (`--color-fill`, `--color-fill-2`): the neutral tag, a skeleton, the selected segment, an avatar.
- **Hover** / **Press** (`--color-hover`, `--color-press`): laid over any surface.

### Lines
- **Line** (`--color-line`): the hairline of a card and between rows.
- **Line Strong** (`--color-line-strong`): a button's edge, a divider that must read.
- **Edge** (`--color-edge`): the boundary of a field, checkbox, radio, switch and choice chip. It is the one line that reaches 3:1 on every surface.

### Text
- **Text** (`--color-text`): headings, numerals, values.
- **Text 2** (`--color-text-2`): running text.
- **Text 3** (`--color-text-3`): labels, captions, placeholders, chart ticks.

### Hues
Each hue has three tokens: `--x` (a fill or a chart mark), `--x-text` (that hue as text, icon or line on a surface) and `--x-soft` (its tint). In the dark scheme `--x-text` is the hue itself or a step lighter; in the light scheme it is a deep version, so lime text on white never happens.

| **Cyan** | series 2, the "second kind" on calendars (약속), links |
| **Orange** | series 3, the deadline flag |
| **Violet** | series 4, focus windows |

Text on any bright hue fill is `--color-on-accent` (near-black green) in both schemes; `--color-on-accent-2` is the quieter step. Text on the red fill is `--color-on-danger`.

### Data series
`--data-1` to `--data-4` are lime, cyan, orange, violet. `--data-rest` is the neutral series (the other days of a week: off-white on dark, ink on light) and `--data-track` an empty bar or the ring's ground. Each series also has `--data-N-edge`: the same colour in the dark scheme, a deeper one in the light scheme, where a lime or cyan fill alone would not reach 3:1 on a white card. A mark takes its edge as a 1px stroke or outline.

### Named Rules
**The One Lime Rule.** A view has one lime thing to do: its primary button. Lime elsewhere on the view is not an action: the current nav item, today's bar, the leading series, a "now" mark. Lime never means ok, caution or information; those are green, amber and sky.

**The Fill Or Text Rule.** A bright hue is either a fill with dark text on it (`--x` under `--color-on-accent`) or text on a surface (`--x-text`). Never `--x` as text in the light scheme, never light text on a bright fill.

**The Red Is Danger Rule.** Red is danger and overdue. A deadline is an orange flag; red arrives when it has passed.

**The Second Signal Rule.** No meaning rides on hue alone. A state is a word in its tag; the selected segment is lifted and heavier; a switch moves its knob; the current nav item carries `aria-current`; a chart's numbers are also text.

## Typography

**Body and Display Font:** Pretendard Variable, bundled (`src/assets/fonts/PretendardVariable.woff2`, v1.3.9, SIL OFL 1.1, weights 45 to 920 in one file), preloaded from `index.html`. Behind it the system Korean sans (Noto Sans CJK KR, Apple SD Gothic Neo, Malgun Gothic) and `system-ui`. No font is requested from the network.
**Mono Font:** `ui-monospace`, D2Coding, JetBrains Mono, Noto Sans Mono CJK KR. Only where the content is code.

**Character:** One family carries everything; rank is size, weight and the quiet around a thing. Large numerals are light (300) and tabular; headings are medium (500); body is 16px regular. Nothing is set in capitals.

### Hierarchy
The scale is fixed: 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 22, 28, 32px, plus numerals at 40 and 56px and the focus timer's display size. Weights are 300, 400, 500, 600 and 650.

- **Display** (`--text-display`, 4.5rem, 7.5rem from 1280px): the focus timer and nothing else. Calm mode has its own step (`--text-display-calm`).
- **Numeral Large** (`--text-num-lg`, 56px, 300, tabular): a timer inside a card.
- **Numeral** (`--text-num`, 40px, 300, tabular, `.numeral`): the one big number of a card. Its unit (`.numeral-unit`) is half the size and a step quieter.
- **Headline** (`--text-3xl`, 32px, 500): the page's `h1`.
- **Figure** (`--text-2xl`, 28px, 300, tabular, `.stat-value`): a number in a row of figures.
- **Title Large** (`--text-xl`, 22px, 500): dialog titles, a block's title.
- **Title** (`--text-lg`, 18px, 500): section headings, settings legends.
- **Body** (`--text-md`, 16px, 400, line-height 1.6): running text, fields, a card's title (500).
- **Body Small** (`--text-sm`, 14px): buttons, nav items, tables, meta lines.
- **Label** (`--text-xs`, 13px, 500): field labels, tags, captions.
- **Grid Label** (`--text-2xs`, 12px): chart ticks, key caps, the bottom nav. Not for running text.

Below 1024px small text is 15px and the two numeral sizes step down (36 and 48px).

### Named Rules
**The One Face Rule.** One family. A new role is a size and a weight from the scale. Mono is for code only.

**The Light Numeral Rule.** A number that leads a card is light and large; it is never bold. Times, durations and counts are tabular so they line up.

**The Whole Word Rule.** Hangul words do not break mid-word (`word-break: keep-all`); long ids and URLs may wrap anywhere. Headings balance their lines.

## Layout

From 1024px a sidebar (15rem; 4.5rem folded to icons) floats at the left, `--shell-gap` (8px) off the window's edges, and the canvas scrolls beside it. The canvas opens with the top bar, a floating bar of glass that stays at the top while the page moves under it. Below 1024px the sidebar is replaced by a floating bottom nav (3.75rem plus the safe area) and the top bar scrolls away with the page.

- **Sidebar:** the brand (a link to 개요), then the seven places in the order of the loop (`apps/web/FLOW.md`) — 개요, 할 일, 계획, 플래너, 집중, 분석, 노트 — and at the foot 설정 and 도움말. No groups, no action button: putting something down is the capture bar's job, signing out is in the account menu. `src/layout/navigation.ts` is the one list; the sidebar, the bottom nav, the phone's 더 보기 sheet, the breadcrumb, the shortcuts and the search read it. A place owns its sub-screens (할 일 owns a folder's page and a task's, 계획 owns 다시 맞추기, 집중 owns the timer), and stays lit while one of them is open.
- **Top bar:** sidebar toggle and breadcrumb (place, or place / page) at the left; the capture bar in the middle (from 1024px); the now pill, notifications and the account at the right. It holds no page action. The account menu holds the theme, 설정 and 로그아웃.
- **Page:** `.page` is 46rem wide, `.page--wide` 84rem, starting at the canvas's left edge. Page padding is 16px, 24px from 640px, 32px from 1024px. A page opens with its title at the left and its actions at the right, with no rule under it.
- **Dashboard grid:** 12 columns, 16px gaps (12px below 1024px). A dashboard page is a size container (`container: <name> / inline-size`) and its cards change span by the canvas's width, not the window's: three columns from 66rem, two from 44rem, one below. Cards in a row stretch to one height; what a card ends with sits on its floor (`margin-top: auto`).
- **Sticky asides** use `top: var(--sticky-top)` so they clear the top bar.

Spacing is a 4px scale (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64px). A card's padding is 24px (20px below 1024px); inside it 16 to 20px separate its parts. Control heights are 32, 40 and 44px; on a coarse pointer every control is at least 44px.

Breakpoints in use: 480, 640, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440px, the container steps 44rem and 66rem, plus `pointer: coarse`.

### On a phone (HOLO-07)
The server's web is installable: `public/manifest.webmanifest` (standalone, upright, opens 개요), icons rendered from the brand mark by `scripts/render-app-icons.mjs`, and a `theme-color` tag that `theme/theme.ts` keeps on the scheme's ground (`theme/themeColor.ts` — the two grounds as hex, held to the tokens by a test) so the browser's bar and an installed app's status bar are the page's own surface. There is **no service worker and no cache**: off its network the installed app is the page, with the page's offline state. The desktop bundle carries none of it (`config/installHead.ts`).

- **The device's edges.** The page asks for the whole screen (`viewport-fit=cover`); the four insets are the tokens `--safe-top/right/bottom/left` and only `tokens.css` reads `env(safe-area-inset-*)`. The shell pads by them; the phone's nav, a sheet, a dialog, a toast, the canvas's selection bar and the sticky save bar stand on `--safe-bottom`.
- **The keyboard takes its space from the page** (`interactive-widget=resizes-content`): a sheet and its buttons stand on the keyboard. While a field has the caret and the window is that short (`min-aspect-ratio: 3/5`, coarse pointer) the phone's nav steps away, so it is never under a typing thumb.
- **Behind a sheet the page stays put** (coarse pointer): the root does not scroll and does not pull-to-refresh while a `.dialog-backdrop` is open; a dialog contains its own overscroll.
- **No zoom on focus:** under a coarse pointer every field's text is 16px at least (a floor in `base.css`). Pinch zoom is never disabled.
- **Targets:** on a coarse pointer every tap target is 44px (`--hit-target`) in both directions wherever the layout lets it; the last of them are in `styles/touch.css`, the stylesheet's final layer, which holds nothing a mouse sees. The two that stay smaller are grids of time (a week's block, a half-hour cell), at the 24px floor.
- **Gestures are said, not found.** A finger cannot hover: where a surface has a gesture of its own (할 일: long press to pick, hold and drag to move; a schedule: tap a block to change it) a line over the surface says it, on a coarse pointer only. No affordance waits for a hover.
- **A touch the page answered ends there.** A surface that acts on `pointerup` (the canvas) cancels that touch's `touchend`, so the browser's follow-up mouse events and click do not land on whatever the action just put under the finger.
- **Toasts** stand at the top while a dialog or a sheet is open below 1024px: a tall dialog ends in its buttons.
- **플래너** opens on the day for a phone that arrives at its bare address (`plannerPhoneEntry`); the week is one tap away.
- `scripts/touch-walk.mjs` walks the loop with touch on Chromium and WebKit and measures all of this (`docs/ui-polish/README.md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Three layers (FLOW.md §6, HOLO-04): **ambient light**, **content**, **glass**. What floats is glass; what is read is solid.

1. **Ambient light** (`.ambient`): the ground with a very faint lime light from the upper left and a cyan one from the lower right. It does not move. It is what the glass has to bend, and it is faint enough that quiet text on the ground keeps 6:1 at its brightest point.
2. **Content**: cards. A card is one step up from the ground with a hairline edge and is opaque — numbers and running text are never set on glass. In the dark scheme it has a faint sheen from its top-left corner (`--card-sheen`); in the light scheme it is plain white. A well (`--color-inset`) is one step down, inside a card.
3. **Glass** (`.glass`): the sidebar, the top bar and what it holds, menus and popovers, the planner's detail card, dialogs and sheets, toasts, the phone's nav, segmented controls, the pane over the glass object — and, on the 할 일 canvas, the floating to-dos, the folders and the one floating detail.

### Glass (`.glass`, `src/styles/primitives.css`)
One primitive, used as a class beside the component's own. Its values are the `--glass-*` tokens.

- **Two tiers (HOLO-06).** An engine can claim `backdrop-filter` and still not paint it (the WebKit the desktop shell runs on, without accelerated compositing: the canvas's detail card was see-through and the to-dos behind it were written across its words). So no glass depends on the blur:
  - **Overlay** — `.glass` as it is: everything that floats over content and can have text behind it (the canvas's detail, menus and popovers, the capture panel, dialogs and sheets, toasts, the planner's item card, the notifications panel, the task picker, the sticky save bar, and the two bars the page scrolls under: the top bar from 1024px and the phone's nav). Its fill is nearly opaque (`--glass-fill-over`: 94% in both schemes) and does the work alone: text on it is AA and what is written behind it comes through at no more than **1.2:1** — a shade, not words. The blur is a bonus on top.
  - **Chrome** — `.glass--chrome` (with the filter) and `.glass--still` (without): glass that only ever has the ambient light or a card behind it (the sidebar, a segmented control, the top bar below 1024px, the sign-in card, a panel set on a card, a to-do and a folder on the canvas). It stays airy (`--glass-fill` 72% dark / 76% white, `--glass-fill-still` 62% / 66%).
- **Body:** what is behind shows through blurred (24px) and saturated, and is brought toward the scheme's ground — dimmed in the dark scheme (`brightness(0.3)`), lifted in the light one (`contrast(0.5) brightness(1.5)`) — under the tier's fill. That is how the glass "darkens itself" when something bright slides under it. The fill is the scrim text sits on.
- **Edge:** a hairline (`--glass-edge`), an inner highlight along the upper edge and a shade along the lower one (`--glass-highlight`), and a faint sheen from the upper left. Under a mouse the sheen follows the pointer (`theme/glassLight.ts`); that is an enhancement and nothing depends on it.
- **Shape:** corners are concentric with what the glass holds: an item's radius is the surface's radius less its padding (sidebar 20 → items 10; menu 14 → items 8; segmented 10 → tiles 7).
- **Variants:** `.glass--chrome` is the airy body with the filter; `.glass--still` is the airy body with no backdrop filter (the sidebar, a segmented control, the top bar below 1024px where it scrolls with the page), and with `.glass--over` it takes the overlay's fill, still without a filter (the canvas's selection bar, which floats over the to-dos). `.glass--host` puts the material on a layer behind the content so the surface can hold other glass (the top bar and its menus). `.glass--low` is the short shadow of the sidebar and the bars; `.glass--flat` has none (glass set on a card).
- **Glass in glass is solid:** a `.glass` inside another (other than a host) takes the opaque material. One refraction at a time.

**Guarantees** (`test/holo-material.spec.ts` computes them from `tokens.css`):

| On glass, worst case behind it | Dark | Light |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overlay, no filter painted:** Text / Text 2 / Text 3 | 12.3 / 8.2 / 5.0:1 | 15.4 / 9.8 / 6.1:1 |
| **Overlay, no filter painted:** lime text / danger text / link | 10.4 / 6.6 / 9.7:1 | 7.1 / 6.1 / 7.5:1 |
| **Overlay:** what is written behind it, against the glass beside it (at most 1.2:1) | 1.18:1 | 1.14:1 |
| Airy fill with its filter: Text / Text 2 / Text 3 | 12.1 / 8.1 / 4.9:1 | 12.5 / 7.9 / 4.9:1 |
| Airy fill with its filter: lime text / danger text / link | 10.2 / 6.4 / 9.6:1 | 5.7 / 4.9 / 6.1:1 |
| The opaque material, Text 3 | 6.3:1 | 6.9:1 |

- **Contrast:** every text token is at least 4.5:1 on overlay glass whatever is behind it — white, black, or any fill of the app — with the sheen at its brightest and **with no filter painted**; with the filter it is the same or better. Chrome glass is AA over what it sits on (the ambient light at its brightest, a card), also with no filter.
- **Bleed-through:** the theme's strongest text colour, the lime, white and black, written behind overlay glass, differ from the glass beside them by at most 1.2:1. The airy fills let the same text through at 2.5:1 (dark) and 1.7:1 (light): that is why nothing that floats over content may use them.
- **No transparency:** the material is the opaque raised surface (`--glass-solid`) where `backdrop-filter` is missing (`@supports`), under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, and when the root carries `data-transparency="reduce"` (설정 › 화면). An airy fill alone does not hold the contrast over a bright backdrop, so it is only ever used where the backdrop is the app's own ground or a card.
- **Stillness:** under `prefers-reduced-motion` the pointer's light is not installed and overlays do not animate. The ambient light never moves.
- **Cost:** a backdrop filter is on the top bar (from 1024px), the phone's nav (below it), and at most one floating thing at a time (a menu, a popover, a dialog, the canvas's detail) plus a toast while it shows. The sidebar, every segmented control and everything that floats on the 할 일 canvas — two hundred to-dos, twenty folders, the selection bar — are still glass and cost nothing. Only `.glass` may blur; a test fails any other `backdrop-filter` or `filter: blur()`. Refraction (bending what is behind) is not used: it exists in one engine only.

### Named Rules
**The Layer Rule.** Glass floats, content is solid. A card never blurs and never becomes translucent; a floating surface is never an opaque box of its own making — it takes `.glass`. Lime stays the signal on glass too: one lime thing per view. A to-do on the canvas floats, so it is glass, and what it carries is a label — a title and a few words — never a number to be read or a paragraph.

**The No Glow Rule.** No drop shadow on a card, no glow behind a number, no gradient text. The only gradient fill is the current nav item (lime to cyan) and the brand tile.

## Shapes

Corners are generous and consistent: key caps and checkboxes 4px, tags 6px, controls 10px, wells and menus 14px, cards and dialogs 20px. Round shapes are the switch, bar ends, legend dots, the avatar and a radio.

Lines are 1px. The 2px weight is kept for marks that are data: the now line and chart strokes. A dashed line means "not real yet": offline, a day that has not come.

### The Canvas: 할 일 (HOLO-05, `components/canvas`, `domain/canvas`)
할 일 is the product's signature screen: a wide, calm surface on which every open to-do floats. Nothing is in rows. The inbox is the open water; a folder is a dish on it.

- **Built in the DOM, not in WebGL.** A to-do is a real element — named, focusable, in the tab order, with Korean text that is read, clicked, dragged and edited. Its place is one CSS transform, written by one small loop (`TodoCanvas.vue`); the arithmetic is pure and tested (`domain/canvas/*.ts`).
- **The surface** (`.pond`): a well (`--color-inset`) under the same ambient light as the page, hairline edge, 20px corners. It is as tall as the window leaves it, and grows only by the density rule below.
- **A to-do** (`.pond-todo` › `.pond-capsule`): a capsule of **still glass** (`glass glass--still glass--flat`: tint, edge and sheen, no backdrop blur). A mark, the title on one line (ellipsis past 13.5rem), and quietly what matters:
  - **Depth.** A closer deadline is nearer: due within two days (or past) is the largest and brightest at the edge and casts the short glass shadow (46px, 16px type); within a week is the middle (42px, 14px medium); further off or undated is the far one (38px, 14px regular, text 2).
  - **Deadline, in the fewest words:** 오늘, 내일, N일 남음, a date beyond two weeks; within the week in the deadline's orange, further off quiet, and past it N일 지남 in red — the only red on the surface.
  - **Mark:** a ring (to do), a ring with a dot (under way), a lime tick in a square (picked), a green tick (done, on its way out).
  - **In a plan:** the planner's own mark for a block, a small lime bar.
- **Motion: moored, not roaming.** Each to-do is anchored to the place it was left and sways a few pixels around it (7px airy, 4px compact, 60% of that on a narrow surface; one period is 9 to 17 seconds, with a lean of at most 0.7°). The sway is a closed form of time, so it never drifts and the still picture is the same picture. Bodies are kept a gap apart at their anchors (22px airy, 12px compact) — more than twice the sway — so nothing ever touches.
  - **Near the pointer things hold still** (still within 28px, free from 180px; quick to settle, slow to wake). A to-do in focus, picked, open or in the hand is still.
  - **Soft separation.** What is dropped on something eases it aside; the open water's to-dos keep out of every dish; nothing leaves the surface.
  - **Nothing moves** under `prefers-reduced-motion` or in a hidden tab, and no frame is asked for. A frame governor thins the motion before it stutters (full → every other frame without the lean → still) and never steps back up within a visit.
  - **Arriving and leaving.** A to-do put down while the canvas is open arrives from above with its edge lit for a moment; a finished one shows its tick, rises a little and is gone. Both are transforms and opacity, 520ms.
- **Direct manipulation.** Drag to move — the place is remembered per owner on this device (`composables/canvasMemory.ts`, a declared owner slot: ids and fractions of the surface, never a title). A click, or Enter, opens the **floating detail**.
- **Floating detail** (`TodoDetailCard`, `.pond-card glass`): the screen's one frosted surface, anchored to its to-do (right, else left, else under, else over). Title edited in place, 마감 set / changed / cleared, 폴더 picked, then 완료 · 집중 시작 · 계획에 넣기 as secondary buttons, 작업 열기 as a link and 삭제 as a danger ghost. Never a filled button. Escape and 닫기 give the focus back; a press on the water, or opening another, closes it.
- **Selection.** Shift-click, a lasso drawn on the water (a dashed lime outline), Space, Shift+arrow, Ctrl+A; under a finger, a long press. A picked capsule takes the lime edge and a lime tint over the opaque material, and its mark becomes a ticked square. The screen's **one filled button** — 계획에 넣기 in the head — carries the selection to the plan flow (`/plan/schedule?taskIds=…`); a bar of still glass at the foot of the surface offers 폴더로 옮기기, 완료 and 선택 풀기.
- **Folders** (`.pond-folder`): a dish of still glass, 20px corners, tinted faintly with its hue (`projectHue`, the same hue the folder has everywhere), with a 44px head: hue dot, name, count, fold, menu. A folder is a **real project**.
  - Dragging a to-do into a dish files it (its edge turns dashed lime while one is held over it: dashed is "not real yet"); dragging it out returns it to the inbox. What is filed is **put away**: drawn at the compact size, and kept in the dish's rows — flowed like words into lines, in the order they were left, each row's slack to alternate sides so it is not a table. A dish is as large as the capsules it holds need (264px wide up to three, 404px beyond; six rows at most, then `+N개`), sways less (3px, no lean) and is always tidy: nothing in it is ever on top of anything.
  - The name is a button: renaming is in place. The head is the handle that moves the dish.
  - **Folded**, a folder is one pill with its name and count; its to-dos are not drawn.
  - The menu: 이 폴더를 계획에 넣기, 이름 바꾸기, 폴더 페이지 열기, 폴더 없애기. Removing asks first, moves every to-do (finished ones too) to the inbox, then archives the empty folder; the toast offers 되돌리기. Nothing is deleted.
  - The inbox has no dish and no label: it is the water.
- **Density** (`planDensity`). (1) Everything fits with air: full size, the window's height. (2) It fits closer together: the compact size. (3) It needs more room: the surface grows, up to two windows (three on a narrow surface, which scrolls anyway). The folders stand in one column down the right edge: when the column would be taller than the window, every dish becomes a wide one (two capsules to a row), then each gives up a little air, and only then is the surface as tall as the column. The rule counts only the open water beside it. (4) More still: the most urgent are shown and the rest become one **N개 더 보기** at the foot; pressing it lets the surface grow as long as it must (never more than 300 bodies). A dish holds what its size can show and says `+N개` for the rest. A field never seeded before puts the most urgent in the middle of the first window; a handful of to-dos gather there instead of spreading to the edges.
- **Narrow** (under 560px of its own width): a vertical field — the open water as tall as its to-dos ask for, then the dishes stacked under it at full width.
- **Under a finger** (HOLO-07): a swipe scrolls the page, also when it starts on a to-do. A tap opens the detail. A to-do the finger rests on (280ms) is lifted — the device ticks where it can — and from there a drag moves it, while letting go without having moved **picks** it: the touch way to pick several. While anything is picked a tap picks and unpicks instead of opening; a tap on the water lets go. The gestures are written in one line over the surface.
- **Without a pointer or sight it is a list.** One `listbox` (many can be picked), a `group` for the inbox and one for each folder, an `option` a to-do, named in full (title, state, deadline, plan, where it is). One tab stop. Arrows go to the nearest to-do that way, Home and End to the first and last in reading order, Enter opens, Space picks, `v` opens 폴더로 옮기기 (a menu of the inbox, the folders and a new one), Alt+arrow moves the to-do. The keys are written under the surface for a fine pointer. What happens is said in a polite live region.
- **목록** (`TodoListView`): the visible switch beside 폴더 만들기 (캔버스 | 목록, a segmented control) shows the same to-dos as rows — the inbox with the one-line quick add and 말로 여러 개 적기, then each folder as a section, finished to-dos included. The choice is remembered. In the list the filled button is the quick add's, and 계획에 넣기 is a secondary one.
- **Empty.** With nothing put down the surface says so and offers 할 일 담기, which opens the capture bar — the one filled button then.
- **Offline** the surface can still be arranged (where things are is the device's), but nothing is filed, finished, renamed or removed.
- **Cost, measured** (this laptop, headless Chromium, 1440×900): 31, 99 and 201 floating to-dos all hold 16.6ms a frame (p95 16.7, max 16.8) with no long task; settling a full surface of 106 bodies takes about 11ms.

## Motion

Short and functional. Durations are 120, 180 and 260ms, plus 520ms (`--duration-draw`) for a chart drawing in, on `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` or the softer `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.

- **Charts:** bars grow from the floor once; ring arcs and the trend fade in once. Numbers do not count up.
- **The timer ticks.** That is its motion.
- **Overlays:** the scrim fades in (by colour, so the dialog's glass sees the page from its first frame); a dialog, sheet, toast or menu fades in while settling 4 to 16px into place.
- **Layout switch:** panels fade in by opacity only (a transform would trap the Focus calm-mode overlay).
- **Controls:** colour and edge changes take 120ms; a button moves 1px when pressed.
- **Waiting:** the spinner and the skeleton shimmer loop only while something is loading.
- **The 할 일 canvas sways.** It is the one place where something moves without a change of state: slowly, a few pixels, by transform only, still near the pointer and wherever motion is not wanted (see The Canvas).
- **Never** animate a layout property (width, height, padding, grid tracks). The sidebar folds at once.
- **Reduced motion:** every duration token becomes 0, animations and transitions are cut, the spinner and skeleton stop, the glass object holds one frame, and nothing on the 할 일 canvas sways.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour, size, edge and duration from `src/styles/tokens.css`.
- **Do** compose a screen from cards of related sizes on the 12-column grid, with one big light number per card.
- **Do** give every card a title and, where another screen owns the detail, the open icon-link.
- **Do** keep one lime-filled button per view or dialog; other actions are secondary or ghost.
- **Do** compute every number from the user's real data, and show a real empty state when there is none.
- **Do** set text on a bright hue fill in `--color-on-accent`, and use `--x-text` for a hue as text.
- **Do** give every chart a text alternative and every colour-coded mark a second signal.
- **Do** check any new text, tag or mark pair in both schemes before adding a token.
- **Do** keep controls at 44px or more on coarse pointers and the focus ring visible.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a drop shadow, a glow, gradient text, a blur or translucency to a card. Don't write `backdrop-filter` anywhere but the `.glass` primitive, and don't set numbers or running text on glass.
- **Don't** use lime for ok, caution or information, or red for anything but danger and overdue.
- **Don't** set lime (or any bright hue) as text on a light surface.
- **Don't** read `prefers-color-scheme` in a stylesheet; key on `data-scheme`.
- **Don't** invent a metric, a placeholder statistic or a decorative count-up.
- **Don't** add a chart or icon library, or fetch a font or image from the network. three.js is the one 3D library, for the glass object only: it stays behind a dynamic import, and nothing that carries text is drawn with it.
- **Don't** set a size or weight outside the scale, or set text in capitals.
- **Don't** animate a layout property, or anything that is not a change of state (the canvas's sway is the one exception, and it stops near the pointer and under reduced motion).
- **Don't** bring in a name, logo, wording, image or exact screen from another product.
